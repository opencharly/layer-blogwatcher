## Scope note — how these were found, and two ways I got it wrong first

Two further mistakes of mine surfaced while fixing them, both the same species as the search-shape problem:

1. **A regex written to the shape I expected** — `- Bundled by: ...` — missed `- Bundle: ...` and a `(metalayer)` suffix. Widened to match the *reference*, not the line's presumed form.
2. **Editing the wrong file** — four repos in the batch kept returning "unchanged" because their hit is in `charly.yml`, not the README my loop assumed.
